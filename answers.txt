        -It is easier to collaberate with other people when all the tasks are broken down and displayed on Jira

Role and Responsibilities of Scrum Master and Product Owner:
    -The scrum master is focus on making sure the team follows scrums principles to make sure development goes smoothly, 
    so doing things such as daily scrums and spring reviews to make sure the team is able to do their work effienciently.
    -The product owner is in charge of so they are the ones setting what the epics and stories are and what tasks should be completed in each sprint.
    they are making sure that the team is working on effiencent tasks.
